Support

A problem, an idea? We will get back to you.

Contact the studio

Tell us your phone model and what happened: we usually reply within 48 hours.

I changed phones, how do I get my game back?

Sign in to the same iCloud (iPhone, iPad) or Google Play Games (Android) account, then open the game: it offers to restore your save.

My purchase doesn't show up

In the shop, tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't work, write to us with the purchase date (the App Store or Google Play receipt is enough).

I'm stuck on a level

Every level has a solution, checked automatically. Undo a few moves (↶), wait for Rico's hint, or open the secret locker for an extra shelf.

How do I stop ads between levels?

The No ads purchase removes them for good. Videos to earn rewards stay optional.

How do I change my ad choices?

In the game: Settings › Ad choices. See also the privacy policy.
