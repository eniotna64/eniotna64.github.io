LITTLE ORBITS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Who we are

Little Orbits is a puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android, published by Antoine Bourgoin, under the studio name “enioS” (“we”). We are the controller of the data described here.

For any question about your data: antoinebrg6@gmail.com.

In short: the game never asks you for an account or an email address. Your progress stays on your phone, with a copy in your own iCloud or Google Play Games account. Other data that leaves your device is used for advertising, purchases, leaderboards and usage statistics that do not identify you.

What the game keeps on your device

  • your progress: completed levels, stars, best times, achievements, observatory items, chests, missions, daily challenge streaks;
  • your nickname (optional), your credit balance and the list of purchases already credited;
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration, language, zen mode, daily challenge reminder.

The daily challenge reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone: no data is sent for it.

Cloud save

So you don't lose your game when you change phones, a copy of your progress is stored in your iCloud account (iPhone, iPad) or in Google Play Games Saved Games (Android). This copy is kept by Apple or Google in your account, not by us: we have no access to it.

Leaderboards

If you are signed in to Game Center (Apple) or Google Play Games, your scores (stars, completed levels) are sent to these services for the leaderboards. Your player name there is the one of your Game Center or Play Games account, managed by Apple or Google.

Advertising

Little Orbits shows videos you choose to watch to earn rewards (credits, chests, hints, helpers), and occasionally an ad between two levels, which the “No ads” purchase removes. They are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in Europe), which processes for this purpose:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, Android advertising ID), only if you allow it;
  • your IP address and the approximate location it indicates;
  • technical information: device model, operating system, language;
  • your interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data.

This data is used to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Google processes it under its own rules: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Your consent. On first launch, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a message asks whether you accept personalized ads. On iPhone, iOS also asks whether Little Orbits may track your activity. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. You can change your mind at any time: in the game, Settings › Ad choices; on iPhone, also in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Statistics and crash reports

To improve the game, Little Orbits may use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They receive an app installation identifier (not your name), technical information about the device, game events (for example: level started or completed, stars, rewarded ad watched, purchase) and, if the app crashes, a technical report.

  • Statistics only start after the consent message, and are never used for advertising: no advertising identifier is sent to Firebase.
  • Crash reports are only used to fix bugs.

See the Firebase privacy information.

In-app purchases

Credit packs, the starter pack and the “No ads” purchase are bought through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). Payment happens on their side: we never see your name, your card or your email address. Each purchase is verified by the App Store or Google Play before being credited. Apple and Google process your purchases under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

The “Share my win” and “Invite a friend” buttons open your phone's share sheet, with an image and a text you choose to send. Nothing is sent without your action, and we receive nothing.

Children

Little Orbits is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Retention

Game data stays on your device as long as the game is installed, and in your iCloud or Google Play Games account until you delete it. Retention periods for Google AdMob and Firebase are set by Google (see its rules above).

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access to your data, its correction or deletion, object to its processing and withdraw your consent at any time. Since the game sends us no identity data, most of these rights are exercised directly in the app (Settings › Erase progress, Ad choices), in your iCloud or Google Play Games settings, or with Google.

For any request: antoinebrg6@gmail.com. You may also contact your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the game gets a new feature. The date at the top shows the latest version; if anything important changes, the game will let you know.
